# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **CI/CD: merges to `main` deploy to Vercel production.** New `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (push to `main`, pull requests test-only, manual runs): `npm ci`, fetch the compressed licensed model pack from the private Vercel Blob store `cel-village-models` (`tools/fetch-licensed-models.mjs`, `npm run models:fetch`), `npm run test:ci`, `npm run deploy:dist`, `vercel deploy dist --prod`, then a check that the public production URL returns 200. `tools/upload-licensed-models.mjs` (`npm run models:upload`) rebuilds and uploads the pack from a local `dist`; `tools/ci-bootstrap.sh` does the one-time repo and secrets setup. `tools/publish-main.sh` publishes local `main` to GitHub as snapshot commits on `github-main` (no local history, no `shots/`, fast-forward only). `vercel.json` disables Vercel Git deploys so each merge deploys once. Models stay out of git (`.models-pack/`, `.env*` and `.vercel/` added to `.gitignore`). Docs: `docs/CI-CD.md`.
- **Merge: roads + river + coaster + skins (village/grok-merge).** One branch from the deployed 7c6d639 merging `village/grok-roads`, `grok-river`, `grok-coaster` and `grok-skins` in that order (`--no-ff`). Only CHANGELOG.md, package.json scripts and the `world-plan.js` imports conflicted; all were resolved by keeping both sides. `residents.js`/`outskirts-life.js` merged cleanly: roads routes walkers through `src/roads/walking.js`, and river's `bridgeDeck()` deck height stays. After the river merge every road centreline and every sidewalk-graph node and edge at seeds 831 and 0 is dry or on one of the 5 bridges (3 town bridges + 2 footbridges); no regional road comes near the new reaches. Road audit: 0 open (14 justified). Collision sim: 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 over 5400 samples. 169 node tests and `test:checks` pass. Stills are in `shots/grok-merge/` (`tools/shots-grok-merge.mjs`).
- **Kids' ballgame on the Astra Owls field (grok-merge).** Eleven kids (adult townsfolk rigs at 0.64 scale, about the child rig's 1.1 m) play a looping pickup game: pitcher, catcher, batter, four infielders, two outfielders and two waiting by the backstop. Each 12 s play is a pitch, a swing, a white ball in flight, a fielder chase and throw to first; the batter runs to first, and every third play is a big hit and the batter rounds the bases. Weekdays 15:00-19:12 (after school), weekends 09:30-19:12; kids walk over from the school door on the sidewalk graph (no carriageway crossings on that route), walk home at dusk, and count as pedestrians for traffic. Pure clock-driven `src/astra/ballgame.js` rendered by `civic.js`, reusing the eight hidden decorative players' slots (now 11; hidden outside game hours). The field already had home plate in the fence corner, square base paths and a mound inside the diamond; it reads as a box only because the default camera aligns the base paths with the screen. New `tests/ballgame.test.mjs` (>= 9 on the field in game hours, 0 at night), `tools/film-grok-baseball.mjs`.
- **Switchyard pump track sits on its pad (grok-merge).** The crowned track surface dipped 12 cm below the dirt pad at its lips, so the solid pad cut through it and the rollers read as troughs carved into the ground. `pumpHeight`/`pumpSurface` (`skatepark-motion.js`) now start from `PUMP.padTop`; walls rise from the pad to the lip, the grass islands and sign are unchanged, and riders still follow `pumpHeight`. New skatepark test: track min surface >= pad top.
- **Roads, sidewalks and traffic safety (grok-roads).** Regional roads are planned as one filleted record per chain (`src/roads/regional.js`) instead of separately bent, side-shifted legs, so joins no longer stack ribbons, loop or spur. Duplicate and dog-leg links (fair, skatepark, harbor/coast, airport, coast lane) and the bus loop's duplicate avenues are re-authored. Links are placed at full verge width so stops, props and trees clear the final road; the harbor road leaves the bandstand gap to a town lane; and the Highway 4 inlet crossing is drawn as a bridge. Residents and errand walkers now route on a kerb-side sidewalk graph that crosses only at junctions and crosswalks (`src/roads/pedestrian.js`, `src/roads/walking.js`), with sidewalk strips and zebra crossings drawn. Parked cars sit in marked bays. Walkers wait for a clear crossing, and regional cars yield to people, follow the car ahead and take turns at shared junctions. New `tools/audit-roads.mjs` (`docs/ROAD-AUDIT.md`), `tools/sim-collisions.mjs` and `npm run test:grok-roads` (audit clean; a full simulated day at seed 831 with zero car-car, car-person and person-on-road events). Spec: `docs/specs/GROK-ROADS.md`.
- River system (grok-river): the Willow River no longer stops at both ends.
  - It rises as a cascade on the Mountain Pass peak with a plunge pool, keeps its original town bed and bridges, and loops through the west meadow into Stillwater Lake. It leaves the lake as the Stillwater Outflow and widens into a braided delta. The delta cuts through the beach in a new estuary notch in the mainland coast, and its surface slopes down to sea level so it blends into the Gulf.
  - One compiler plan (`src/compiler/water.js`, `town.water`) drives the rendering (`src/astra/water.js`), foliage and scenery exclusion, two new timber footbridges (meadow trail and lake loop) and walker deck heights.
  - Road change: none. Paths are unchanged. `residents.js` and `outskirts-life.js` read deck height from `bridgeDeck()`, and `prop()` rejects river boxes, which dropped a flower bed that sat in the river.
  - The lake surfaces drop by about 5 cm to the plan's lake level. Mainland hills keep 6 m off the banks. The outskirts woods take more placement draws (1600 to 2200) to refill around the river.
  - New `npm run test:river`, `shots:river` and `map:river`. Spec: `docs/specs/GROK-RIVER.md`.
- Fix Copperhead's stray dark "cross ties": the ink hull for each fairground mesh was cached on `geometry.userData`, which three's `BufferGeometry.clone()` shares by reference, so mirrored down-slope pieces and their unmirrored up-slope twins drew one another's outline (a dark track shape sloping the wrong way). Hulls are now cached per geometry in a `WeakMap` and mirrored clones get their own `userData` (`mirrorGeometry`, `hullFor` in `themepark-assets.js`).
- Move Copperhead support placement into `coasterSupports()` in `themepark-coaster.js`; the two curve posts now sit on the rail arc (they were up to 0.24 m off), with the six affected `coaster_support` registry records moved to match. `layCoaster` also returns per-piece `segments` into the centreline.
- Add `tests/themepark-coaster.test.mjs`: pieces join end to end with continuous heading and a closed loop, every raised piece has a support on the lawn under the rail, and a mirrored piece gets its own mirrored hull. Add `tools/shots-grok-coaster.mjs` (port 5216) and spec `docs/specs/GROK-COASTER.md`; before/after stills and a ride clip in `shots/grok-coaster/`.
- Add village skins: swappable looks that restyle the town live, like a CSS theme. Each skin is one data file in `src/skins/` (`cel`, `neon`, `autumn`, `winter`), applied at runtime by `src/astra/skin/` with no reload and no world rebuild. Switch with `?skin=<id>`, the swatch picker at the bottom-left, or K / Shift+K; `__astra.setSkin(id)` also works. The default `cel` skin is all-null and runs the original code path: its pixel diff against 7c6d639 is 0 changed pixels (max delta 0) on wide and downtown views at day and night, both on first load and after switching through every skin and back. Look v2 moved into the `neon` skin (`src/astra/look-v2/` removed; `?look=v2` still opens it). New skins: "Autumn storybook" (rust/amber leaves, harvest ground, amber windows, warm LUT) and "Winter" (shader snow on up-facing surfaces, slush roads, cold keys, warm windows). Draw calls on the wide view, day / night: cel 3679 / 3766, neon 3671 / 3758, autumn and winter 3670 / 3757 (downtown view: cel 2227 / 2283, the others equal or 1 lower). Post passes are paid for by hiding lamp halos. Skins add only data files (< 8 KB each) and 0 network requests. Docs: `docs/SKINS.md` (how to write a skin), spec `docs/specs/GROK-SKINS.md`, log `docs/GROK-SKINS-LOG.md`. Tools: `tools/grok-skins-{shots,film}.mjs`, `tools/grok-skins-diff.py`. Tests: `tests/grok-skins.test.mjs` (11) replaces `tests/grok-style.test.mjs`. Stills and clip are in `shots/grok-skins/`.
