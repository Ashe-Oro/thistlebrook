# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Villager animations.** Town planners and harbor routines now play a shared set of skeletal clips on the original townsfolk wardrobe. Villagers wave in passing, sit at measured bench seats, serve the Corner Cup Café counter and fish from the Saltwind pier. Walking cycles follow distance along the existing paths. TOWN-09 checks seat contact, bind poses and planted feet.

- **Tesla lineup look-alikes and Roadster rest-stop removal (opus-tesla).** The five cars the white Roadster kickflips over are rebuilt so they read as a Cybercab, Cybertruck, Semi, Model Y and Model 3, with no logos or wordmarks. New `src/astra/tesla/lineup-models.js` builds each one from a loft of 10-point cross-sections with vertex-coloured faces (paint, dark-tint glass, black glass roof, trim), plus a smooth-normal ink hull, emissive light bars and one mesh per wheel. The Cybertruck is a single-apex flat-panel wedge with a steel band above the glass, trapezoid arches and light bars at both ends. The Cybercab is a two-door teardrop fastback with no rear window, gold aero discs and full-width bars. The Semi is a cab-over tractor with no trailer: wraparound windshield, roof sweeping into the fairing, tandem axles and fifth wheel. The Model Y is a tall crossover with a light bar and liftgate. The Model 3 is a low grille-less fastback sedan. Lengths follow the real ratios. Paint: stainless, champagne gold, white, red Model Y, deep-blue Model 3. The same factory feeds town traffic. Lineup draw calls fall from 120 to 42 (8 per car, 10 for the Semi), with at most about 2,100 triangles per vehicle. The Roadster model, ramp and jump are unchanged, and the Roadster still clears every car by more than 6 m. `semi.js` is removed. Five shuffled blind critic rounds named all five vehicles correctly every time (log: `docs/specs/OPUS-TESLA-LOG.md`).
  - Rest stop: the bench, crate, barrel, sand pad and footpaths beside the Roadster arena (`district-stop-roadster` in `scenes.json`) are gone, along with the arena's pale connector slab that lay across the east road's crosswalk. The other eight district stops are unchanged.
  - New tests: per-vehicle triangle and draw-call budgets, real length and height ordering, and no arena geometry beyond the lot edge. The rest-stop count is now 8. New `tools/shots-opus-tesla.mjs` captures before/after shots, per-car 3/4 and side renders, the rest-stop camera, `lineup-jump.mp4` and a draw-call/triangle metrics JSON into `shots/opus-tesla/`. Reference photo credits are in `docs/specs/tesla-refs/SOURCES.md`; the photos themselves are not committed.

- **Crosswalks and continuous sidewalks.** Thistlebrook places perpendicular, curb-to-curb zebras beyond junction mouths and closes sidewalk gaps at corners, turns and road ends. Residents and street paint use the same pedestrian graph. The road audit checks crossing geometry, sidewalk continuity and town design rules, and the full-day simulation only allows walkers on asphalt inside graph crossing corridors. See [Town Rules](docs/TOWN-RULES.md) and [before and after views](shots/sol-crosswalks/).

- **Pinewood: woods, trails and hikers in the west meadow (grok-woods).** The empty meadow between the Pine ridge peaks, the Willow River's west loop and downtown (about x -200 to -72; Ashe's `refs/roads/ashe-emptyfield.png`) is now Pinewood. A new foothill ridge (Pinewood Ridge, summit about 15 m) ties the two existing peaks together; its height is zero outside its footprint, so roads, the river, the coast and the fair stay flat. Seven dirt trails (383 m) are draped on the terrain as a two-tone ribbon: Pinewood Trail from a trailhead off the Fair Lane sidewalk, the Lookout switchbacks (about 20% grade), the ridge walk to the South Knob cairn, the knob descent, the Willow River trail from the riverside path (over the existing meadow footbridge), the pond path and the meadow cut. There is a summit lookout deck with benches, Pinewood Pond with reeds and a log bench, a picnic glade, two meadows and two carved trailhead boards. Owned kit only: 718 pines and oaks plus 219 bushes, boulders, mossy logs and features at seed 831, and a thinner west fringe (57 trees and 88 bushes and rocks, own seed) fading out towards the beach so the forest has no hard edge. No crown sits over a trail in the default camera. Hikers are pure and clock-driven (`src/astra/woods-hikers.js`): 12 on weekdays, 22 at weekends, some walking in pairs, on the trails from 08:00 and all off by 17:30 (18:00 at weekends). They keep to the tread, rest at the lookout, cairn or pond, and count as pedestrians in the collision sim. Plan `src/compiler/woods.js`, render `src/astra/woods.js`; `landscape.js` instances now honour a model's ground height so trees sit on the ridge. Draw calls (seed 831, 10:24): Ashe's camera 1089 -> 1213 (+124), village wide 7632 -> 7747 (+115), woods wide 2299 -> 2478 (+179), switchbacks close-up at Saturday 13:40 196 -> 303. Triangles +0.9-1.0 M on those views (instanced trees). `tools/river-map.mjs` reads `water.reaches` again and plots models and trails. New `npm run test:woods` (`tests/grok-woods.test.mjs`), `shots:woods`, `film:woods`. Road audit 0 open (14 justified); collision sim 0 / 0 / 0 / 0 over 5400 samples; 175 node tests and `test:checks` pass. Stills and a hiker clip in `shots/grok-woods/`. Spec: `docs/specs/GROK-WOODS.md`.

- **Kids' ballgame on the Astra Owls field (grok-merge).** Eleven kids (adult townsfolk rigs at 0.64 scale, about the child rig's 1.1 m) play a looping pickup game: pitcher, catcher, batter, four infielders, two outfielders and two waiting by the backstop. Each 12 s play is a pitch, a swing, a white ball in flight, a fielder chase and throw to first; the batter runs to first, and every third play is a big hit and the batter rounds the bases. Weekdays 15:00-19:12 (after school), weekends 09:30-19:12; kids walk over from the school door on the sidewalk graph (no carriageway crossings on that route), walk home at dusk, and count as pedestrians for traffic. Pure clock-driven `src/astra/ballgame.js` rendered by `civic.js`, reusing the eight hidden decorative players' slots (now 11; hidden outside game hours). - **Roads, sidewalks and traffic safety (grok-roads).** Regional roads are planned as one filleted record per chain (`src/roads/regional.js`) instead of separately bent, side-shifted legs, so joins no longer stack ribbons, loop or spur. Duplicate and dog-leg links (fair, skatepark, harbor/coast, airport, coast lane) and the bus loop's duplicate avenues are re-authored. Links are placed at full verge width so stops, props and trees clear the final road; the harbor road leaves the bandstand gap to a town lane; and the Highway 4 inlet crossing is drawn as a bridge. Residents and errand walkers now route on a kerb-side sidewalk graph that crosses only at junctions and crosswalks (`src/roads/pedestrian.js`, `src/roads/walking.js`), with sidewalk strips and zebra crossings drawn. Parked cars sit in marked bays. Walkers wait for a clear crossing, and regional cars yield to people, follow the car ahead and take turns at shared junctions. New `tools/audit-roads.mjs` (`docs/ROAD-AUDIT.md`), `tools/sim-collisions.mjs` and `npm run test:grok-roads` (audit clean; a full simulated day at seed 831 with zero car-car, car-person and person-on-road events). Spec: `docs/specs/GROK-ROADS.md`.
- River system (grok-river): the Willow River no longer stops at both ends.
  - It rises as a cascade on the Mountain Pass peak with a plunge pool, keeps its original town bed and bridges, and loops through the west meadow into Stillwater Lake. It leaves the lake as the Stillwater Outflow and widens into a braided delta. The delta cuts through the beach in a new estuary notch in the mainland coast, and its surface slopes down to sea level so it blends into the Gulf.
  - One compiler plan (`src/compiler/water.js`, `town.water`) drives the rendering (`src/astra/water.js`), foliage and scenery exclusion, two new timber footbridges (meadow trail and lake loop) and walker deck heights.
  - Road change: none. Paths are unchanged. `residents.js` and `outskirts-life.js` read deck height from `bridgeDeck()`, and `prop()` rejects river boxes, which dropped a flower bed that sat in the river.
  - The lake surfaces drop by about 5 cm to the plan's lake level. Mainland hills keep 6 m off the banks. The outskirts woods take more placement draws (1600 to 2200) to refill around the river.
  - New `npm run test:river`, `shots:river` and `map:river`. Spec: `docs/specs/GROK-RIVER.md`.
