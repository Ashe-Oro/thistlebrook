# Villager needs decisions

## Implementation

- Recovered the brief from local commit `c227898`. The provisioned worktree was already at main (`e72f06e`). Kept that worktree and named its local branch `village/life-needs` as the brief requests. Installed dependencies with `npm ci` and linked the shared ignored `public/licensed` tree used by the other lanes.
- Wrote the seed 831 and 42 headless day tests first. The old planner failed the low-food, continuous sleep, and food-shop checks. The day test runs on the game clock (90 simulated seconds per town hour) and starts at 21:00 so the first measured midnight includes the nurse’s commute.
- Hunger changes from a rising deficit to a falling food reserve. Initial reserve is 52–80. Idle hunger previously rose 0.045/s. Food now falls 0.12/s awake and 0.055/s asleep. Meals previously removed 65 points of hunger and now add 65 points of food up to 100. The 55-point trigger and 150-second spacing give residents regular meals without repeated eating at one table. Between 11:30 and 13:30, lunch can start at 75 or below. Home meals use the pantry if the resident has no packed meal. Shops and workplace breaks require packed food. Meals take 14 seconds, previously 25 seconds or the prop duration, so more than one break fits into a shift.
- Walking and work now drain energy at 0.045/s and 0.055/s respectively, compared with 0.028/s walking and 0.012/s for other activities. Idle drain is 0.015/s and the tired threshold is 30, previously 25. An awake rest restores 24 versus 32. Actual sleep restores 0.22/s with a cap at 100. Recreation falls 0.032/s instead of 0.018/s. A reserve below 55 can prompt a visit or chat. Visits restore 22 rather than 12, completed shared games restore 45, and conversations restore 12 for each participant. Sociability at or above 0.7 can prompt a chat even if recreation is high. The existing greeting distance and 70-second cooldown still apply.
- Replaced repeated short rests with one eight-hour home sleep block, normally 22:00–06:00. Early departures move the block earlier, based on sidewalk route length, walking pace and 16 seconds of margin. Luca and Theo are late venue staff and can still be walking home after 22:30. Otis sleeps 07:30–15:30 and walks to his midnight clinic shift. Mae is retired. Market, harbor, fair and district actors retain their own schedules.
- Removed the cumulative work allowance. A shift uses the resident’s actual assigned location until clock-out, with brief packed meals on site. Staff can reach a closed workplace before their shift and work while its public doors are shut. Food purchases still require public hours. Only food businesses sell packed meals, at the existing 300-cent price for two. A purchase canceled by closing transfers neither money nor stock.
- The first full-day run showed that distant picnics could consume several town hours and delay work or bedtime. Impromptu meals now search for picnic seats within 32 m. Outings near bedtime or the next shift use a travel estimate with 40% allowance and 0.4 hours for the stop. Residents begin heading home up to 1.25 hours before bed. Authored afternoon activities have priority over routine errands while food is above 25. This retains the shared chess and practice reservations and leaves the Owls game and other scripted events on their existing controllers.
- If a destination is rejected, the resident records the fallback and excludes that place for 90 simulated seconds. Food searches check public hours and slot availability, then fall back to the household pantry. Neither path failure nor a closing shop moves a resident off the pedestrian graph.
