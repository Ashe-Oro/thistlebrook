# Airfield kit log

Lane: `village/airfield-kit`, run from [OPUS-AIRFIELD.md](../OPUS-AIRFIELD.md). The full spec, `docs/specs/overnight/AIRFIELD-KIT.md`, is on `village/asset-pack-review` rather than main, so this run worked from that copy. Decisions are appended in order.

## 2026-11-20

- **Scale.** Town vehicles use 0.456 units per metre, and the pack authors its buildings at about town scale (hangar 8 × 7 units, Cessna span 4 units). Buildings are placed at 1.25 to 1.6. The terminal stays at 1.3, the spec's limit, and the Cessna is placed at 1.5 (6 units span). The procedural airliner (14 units) and the helicopter keep their sizes and timetable.
- **Layout (airport frame).** On the public side, the terminal FBO faces the car park and the flight club office stands beside it. The apron canopy shelters the walk from the terminal to stand A1. The control tower keeps its old spot, with the GSE shed east of it. The club hangar sits on the old hangar footprint and the T-hangars are north of it. The fuel farm is on a new GA service pad with two tied-down Cessnas. The fire station sits on the south taxi link with its fire tender. Airside, there is a rotating beacon, a PAPI, three apron floodlights, the windsock, an airstair and a GPU at A1, 30 runway edge lights and 15 taxiway lights. Every building footprint stays clear of the runway strip, the taxi lane, both links and the helipad, and of every aircraft's footprint over a full timetable. `tests/airfield-kit.test.mjs` checks this.
- **Draws.** All static pack placements (9 buildings and 7 airside props) are baked into one vertex-coloured geometry, giving one toon draw plus one ink draw. Edge lights and taxiway lights are instanced. Only moving pieces (aircraft, windsock, vehicles) are separate objects.
- **Cessna propeller.** The GLB is one merged mesh. Triangles in front of the cowling (model z > 1.42) are split into a spinner group that rotates about the nose axis.
- **Apron vehicles.** The tug and baggage train loop moved 3 units south (centre z -75). The old loop let the rigid cart train swing within 0.3 units of the airliner's nose at stand. A fuel bowser (`af-fuel-truck`, same GLB as the fire tender, 8 m class) drives an ellipse on the GA pad. It waits at the fuel farm, then pumps beside G-THBK. The follow-me car (`af-follow-me`, 4.5 m class) patrols between stands B1 and B2. No loop reaches the stand-to-taxiway corridor (x ≥ 92). The new test samples 24 town hours and finds no runway use, no aircraft contact, no building contact and no vehicle overlap.
- **Night.** Fixtures go through `landmark-glow.js` (`airfieldFixtures()` in the plan): edge, threshold, taxiway and PAPI halos, a tower cab wash with a red obstruction beacon, green and white beacon beams swept by a follow object, flood cones and pools, and window cards. They use the same night factor as the lamps, so they are on from dusk through dawn (19:00–06:00 is checked) and off at noon. No point lights were added.
- **Kept as surfaces.** The parcel, runway paint, apron, taxi lanes, helipad, approach street, planters, parking bays and the fence are still toon boxes. None of them is a building, and the test caps every remaining box at 2 units tall. Pack runway tiles were not used: their threshold paints a "27", and the runway here is 18/36.
- **Landside cars.** The three box cars in the terminal car park are now pack cars: the Suburban sedan, the Vice Beach pastel sedan and the Suburban minivan.
